SousaFX version 0.11.4 has been released, with just has a couple small improvements from 0.11.3

Check out the updated documentation here: [https://rnbo.sousastep.quest/v0.11.4/](https://sousastep.github.io/SousaFX-rnbo-docs/v0.11.4/)

Release Notes:

- Make delay feedback binding taper linear.

- Don't sent drum delay into drum looper.

- Add talkback mic binding with auto-mute logic.

- Improve default preset.
